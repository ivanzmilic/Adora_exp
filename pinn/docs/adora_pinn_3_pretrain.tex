\docheader{Adora PINN --- Step 3: Pretraining (warm start)}%
{As-built notes \quad$\cdot$\quad Ivan Milic \quad$\cdot$\quad 2026-09-20 \quad$\cdot$\quad step 3 of pinn/plan.md}

Step 3 pretrains the step-1 field to the step-2 atmosphere so the PINN starts warm: a plain supervised fit
of $f_\theta(x,y,z)$ to the stored cube (no RT yet). Files \file{pinn/pretrain.py},
\file{pinn/step3\_pretrain.ipynb}; warm-start weights saved to
\file{/dat/milic/adora\_pinn\_develop/pretrained\_field.npz}.

\textbf{Method.} Random minibatches (8192) of the $128\times128\times56$ grid points; a per-channel balanced
loss --- $P$ compared in $\log_{10}$, the rest scaled ($T/10^3$, $v_z/3{\cdot}10^3$, $B/0.05$,
$v_\mathrm{turb}/10^3$); \code{optax} Adam, cosine LR $3\times10^{-3}$, 6000 steps ($\sim$3\,min CPU, with a
tqdm progress bar). Coordinates use \code{af.grid\_coords}, which matches the cube's $(x,y,z)$ flatten order.

\textbf{Result.} Loss $0.79\to1.0\times10^{-5}$. Full-grid RMS vs the target: $T$ $3.96$\,K ($\sim$0.05\%),
$P$ $4.1\times10^{-3}$\,dex ($\sim$0.9\%), $v_z$ $14$\,m/s (of $\pm3$\,km/s), $v_\mathrm{turb}$ $4$\,m/s,
$B\approx0$. The field reproduces the FAL-C stratification and the granulation-like $v_z$ pattern --- a clean
warm start. The notebook shows the loss curve, field-vs-target profiles ($T,\log P,v_\mathrm{turb},v_z$), and
$v_z$ target/field/residual maps.
